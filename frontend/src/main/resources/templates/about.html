Project Background & Purpose

Automating Waste Segregation with Machine Learning

WasteSort AI was conceived as an engineering capstone project to address one of the most persistent bottlenecks in modern circular economies: source contamination.

The Global Segregation Challenge

Every year, over 2 billion metric tons of municipal solid waste are generated worldwide. Despite widespread availability of recycling facilities, up to 60% of recyclable materials end up incinerated or landfilled due to cross-contamination caused by improper sorting at home and in public facilities.

People often suffer from "wish-cycling" — placing non-recyclable or food-greased items into recycling bins under the assumption that recycling plants will sort it out. In practice, a single greasy pizza box can ruin an entire batch of recycled cardboard pulp.

How Image Classification Solves It

By leveraging Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) trained on thousands of labeled waste images, WasteSort AI provides real-time visual recognition. Users simply capture or upload a picture, and the system extracts geometric contours, material reflectivity, and surface texture to classify the item into its appropriate waste stream.

Taxonomy Guide

The Six Supported Waste Streams

An in-depth guide on what belongs in each category and the essential pre-disposal steps.

1. Glass

Accepted: Clear, brown, and green beverage bottles, pickle and jam jars, condiment containers.

Not Accepted: Broken window glass, drinking tumblers, Pyrex ovenware, ceramic mugs, lightbulbs (these have different melting temperatures).

Target: Cyan / Teal Glass Bin Rinse & remove metal caps

2. Paper

Accepted: Standard printer paper, newspapers, magazines, circulars, envelopes, clean grocery paper bags.

Not Accepted: Thermal receipts (coated in BPA), greasy butter paper, waxed baking parchment, napkins, paper towels.

Target: Blue Paper Bin Must remain dry

3. Cardboard

Accepted: Corrugated shipping cartons, cereal boxes (minus plastic liner), egg cartons, clean shoe boxes.

Not Accepted: Oil-soaked pizza box bases, wax-coated frozen food containers.

Target: Blue / Brown Cardboard Bin Flatten & remove plastic tape

4. Plastic

Accepted: Rigid PET (#1) and HDPE (#2) bottles, milk jugs, shampoo bottles, detergent jugs, clear clamshells.

Not Accepted: Flimsy grocery bags, bubble wrap, plastic utensils, cling film, styrofoam cups.

Target: Yellow Plastic Bin Empty liquids & replace screw-cap

5. Metal

Accepted: Aluminum soda and sparkling water cans, tin food cans, clean aluminum foil trays, metal jar lids.

Not Accepted: Paint cans, aerosol spray cans with propellant remaining, electronic scrap, batteries.

Target: Grey / Silver Metal Bin Quick rinse with water

6. Trash (Non-Recyclable)

Accepted: Composite multilayer snack bags, soiled diapers, pet waste, broken ceramics, wax paper, rubber items.

Not Accepted: Hazardous chemicals, lithium batteries, medical sharps (require specialized municipal drop-offs).

Target: Black / Red Landfill Bin Bag securely before binning
Technical Architecture

System Engineering & Viva Walkthrough

How WasteSort AI is constructed for academic defense and real-world deployment.

A

View Layer (Thymeleaf)

Semantic HTML5 with server-side Thymeleaf templating and reusable header/footer fragments ensures zero frontend dependency bloat and instant rendering.

B

Controller (Spring Boot)

A lightweight Spring MVC Controller maps standard endpoints (/, /classify, /history, /about) and manages multipart file ingestion.

C

Inference Layer (Future)

The image classification inference layer receives the image file, performs 224x224 RGB normalization, and runs feedforward evaluation against the 6 classes.
